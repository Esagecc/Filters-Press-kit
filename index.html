FILTERS

"Filters, an app you do not want open"

Director / Writer Statement

I am so fortunate to start my directing journey with a horror short. As a lifelong fan of the horror genre, I enjoy the adrenaline that a terrifying scene can bring. This film began with a simple idea: we enjoy and rely on our smart devices in so many ways. We have become accustomed to the convenience of carrying our smartphones everywhere we go. But what if the very thing we carry so casually could ultimately become our demise?


I wanted to bring fear into an everyday activity that so many people enjoy and hopefully leave the audience cringing at the dark possibilities behind something so ordinary. Not only was I excited to direct a horror film, but I was also honored to work with two incredibly talented Latina actresses. I believe that a diverse cast and crew bring unique perspectives, energy, and creativity to a production—elements that can make a film more meaningful.


I am especially grateful to have had an amazing producer, Robert Cantu—also known as my husband—by my side. His ability to bring together talented individuals with different skills and experiences made the production process much smoother and allowed us to bring this story to life.


— Elizabeth Cantu

About the Film

Logline

Ofelia and Tina are co-workers alone at work who decide to take a break and start playing with face filter apps, unaware that the face filter app is cursed and deadly.

Synopsis

Two Latina students working a late shift in an empty campus building try to break the eerie silence with ghost stories and a harmless prank. The fun takes a dark turn when they open a horror-themed camera filter app, only for one of the girls to watch her friend transform into a demonic entity. What begins as a glitch turns into a possessed student who hunts her terrified classmate through the dark hallways, ending in a brutal, bloody execution.
